## What the CV can't show

The CV is a formal document, so it lists qualifications and roles. It
can't show the thing that matters most here: that after a seven-and-a-half-
year interrupted degree that ended with a 2.2, I spent nearly thirty
years as a silent architect — designing systems on paper that I couldn't
build — and then, in January 2026, started building them.

The skills I'm using now are the ones I was trained in and never got to
use: object-oriented analysis, system decomposition, contract design,
and the discipline of making complexity legible. The difference is that
I now have AI tools and the time to apply them. The Origin page tells
that story properly.

factory architectures · Markdown and static-site publishing ·
command-line Android builds · Termux · Node-RED · Ollama / local

**The education record:** BSc (Hons) Computing, 2.2, awarded after
