# Pointers for each file

A working reference for editing the garden yourself. One section per
file: what it's for, what to keep, what to watch. Not rules — judgement
calls flagged as such.

---

## origin.md

**Purpose:** the "why." The only page that tells the personal story.
Everything else is evidence or projection.

**Keep:**
- The short version (3 sentences). It's the only thing a non-clicker sees.
- The factual tone on the tragedy chain. Stated, not dwelt on.
- The "silent architect" framing — it earns the thirty years as *work*.
- The named specifics (poker arena, NLP in Pascal, Termux, Qwen) — they're
  the evidence of the claim.
- The closing 4 GB note — it's the memorable fact.

**Watch:**
- Length. It's ~700 words; if it grows past ~900, move the experiments
  detail to `projects-index.md` and link.
- The "felt like nothing" line. It earns the comeback, but it's the one
  sentence that risks reading as self-pity. Keep or cut on instinct.
- Any temptation to add *more* backstory. The origin page is one page,
  not a memoir.

**Edit freely:** order within the "What I built" section; the exact
experiments named.

---

## meta-system.md

**Purpose:** the pitch. This is the page a funder reads, and the one a
technical collaborator scrolls.

**Keep:**
- The opening one-paragraph definition. It's plain-English and it's
  true — resist jargon here.
- The novelty sentence: *"not better AI, but a better envelope around
  the AI."* A funder will repeat this line.
- The "why it's credible" section. It's the load-bearing proof section.
- The "Skip it if you don't need it" gate before the architecture
  section.
- The constraints section at the end — it's a quiet flex.

**Watch:**
- "It is built, not proposed" — true of components, not the full
  consolidation. Keep the status honesty ("the current working system
  includes…") or add a one-line status note if a reader asks.
- The forecast teaser at the end currently lands on "platform" (2030).
  Consider ending it on 2027 ("a working factory") so the front door
  ends on near-term proof.
- Internal vocabulary (atoms, packets, archiver). Fine in the technical
  section; keep it out of the funder sections.

**Edit freely:** the architecture section's depth; the balance of
funder vs. technical content (but always funder-first).

## projects-index.md

**Purpose:** the map. A reader who only reads this page should
understand the shape of the year's work and where to find it.

**Keep:**
- The three-field structure per entry (what it is / what it proves /
  where). The repetition is what makes it scannable.
- META-SYSTEM first (headline), then the two hosted systems (proof),
  then the experiments (learning), then the silent years (context).
- "What it proves" as a distinct field. It's what turns a list into an
  argument.

**Watch:**
- MOE description may undersell it if the hub hosts more than one
  engine. Worth a look before publishing.
- The poker arena entry. It's the most personal and the most likely to
  prompt "why is this here?" It's framed as evidence of the silent
  years — but it's the one to cut if you'd rather it live on Origin
  only.
- The experiments entry is grouped, not individually linked. That's
  right for now; if the YouTube channel gets chapter markers, link
  specific videos later.

**Edit freely:** number of experiments listed; level of detail per
entry. The structure should stay.

## forecast.md

**Purpose:** the pitch for the future. Its credibility comes from the
discipline of "proven by year end," not from the ambition.

**Keep:**
- "Proven by year end" for every year. This is the whole page's
  credibility.
- The "Dependency" line per year. It's honest and it stops the forecast
  from pretending each year stands alone.
- The closing "AI complexity moves upward" rule.
- 2026 marked "in progress, on track" — honesty about the current year
  buys credibility for the later ones.

**Watch:**
- Each year's claim should be *checkable*, not aspirational. If a claim
  becomes vague ("matures," "evolves"), sharpen it or move it.
- The 2030 claim ("platform") is the most ambitious. It's fine as long
  as the earlier years are proven-by-when, because the reader's trust
  comes from the pattern, not from 2030 being plausible on its own.
- If any year slips, update the page. The page says unmet claims will
  be visible — that promise is the page's value.

**Edit freely:** the specific claims per year, as long as each stays
checkable and the dependencies stay honest.

## cv.md

**Purpose:** the formal record. It points to the PDF and covers what the
PDF can't.

**Keep:**
- "What the CV can't show" — names the 2.2, explains it, doesn't
  dwell. This is the honest paragraph a coach will notice.
- Skills listed as *current use*, not proficiency claims. Everything
  listed should be something you're actually using.
- The bare-facts education line, repeated so a reader who reads only
  cv.md gets the honest version.

**Watch:**
- The PDF file must exist at `cv.pdf` and be current. `cv.md` points at
  it.
- Skills list should stay honest. If you stop using something, remove
  it — the credibility comes from "everything here is current."
- Length. If it grows, move detail to Origin, keep cv.md to the pointer
  + what the PDF can't show.

**Edit freely:** skills list; the "what the CV can't show" wording.

## index.html + style.css

**Purpose:** the shell. Renders `.md` files as pages with hash routing.

**Keep:**
- The `pages` map — it's the list of files the shell knows about.
- Hash routing (`#origin`, `#projects`, etc.) — shareable, back-button
  works.
- The `.md`-link rewriting (in-page links become hashes automatically).
- The local-server error message.

**Watch:**
- Adding a page means editing **both** `nav` and the `pages` map. If you
  forget one, the link is dead or the page doesn't load.
- `marked` from CDN is the one external dependency. If you want zero
  dependencies (in the spirit of your "no dependencies beyond the
  necessary" rule), download `marked.min.js` into the folder and change
  the `<script src>` to point at it.
- The CSS is minimal by design. Add styling sparingly; the garden's
  register is "considered, not flashy."

**Edit freely:** colours, fonts, spacing. The structure (nav, main,
content div, hash router) should stay unless you're changing the
approach.

---

## When to run the final check

Before publishing:
1. Run `python -m http.server` and click every nav link and every
   in-page `.md` link. Confirm none are dead.
2. Check the `cv.pdf` link resolves.
3. Read each page as a stranger would: does the funder section stand
   alone? Does the technical section have the depth? Does Origin earn
   the comeback?
4. Then run it by me for a final read.
